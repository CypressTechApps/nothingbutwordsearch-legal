Help & Support

Nothing But Word Search for Android · Last updated 15 October 2026

Get in touch

Write to support@cypresstech.net. Please include the app name, the version from Settings → About, your phone model and what happened. The full rules are in the app at Settings → How to play.

How do I mark a word?

Press on its first letter, drag to its last letter and lift your finger. The line snaps to rows, columns and diagonals, and dragging backwards works too.

Can I make the letters bigger?

Yes. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in, up to three times, and drag with two fingers to move around. Pinch back out to see the whole grid.

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight on your phone. It is made on the phone from the date, so it works offline.

How do I remove the ads?

Settings → Remove ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it removes every ad. It does not unlock anything else, because nothing else is locked.

I bought it but still see ads, or I have a new phone.

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the Google account you bought it with. If the price does not show, check that you are online and that Google Play is up to date.

Do ads ever appear during a game?

No. The game screen never shows an ad. The free version shows a banner on menu screens and may show one full-screen ad after a game has finished.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every part of the game works with no connection. Ads simply do not appear.

Where are my games and statistics kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so they do not move to a new phone and are erased if you uninstall.

I closed the app mid-game. Is it lost?

No. The game is saved after every move. Open the app and tap Continue.

Why did starting a new game count as a loss?

Starting a new game while one is in progress ends the old one and records it as a loss.

How do I change my ad consent?

Where a consent choice applies to you, Settings → Privacy choices opens the form again.
